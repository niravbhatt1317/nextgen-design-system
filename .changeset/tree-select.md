TreeSelect - a pick from a tree, in a dropdown: groups that fold their items away, the items, and what sits under each item, every level a checkbox. A group's checkbox picks every item under it and its words fold it; an item's words pick it whole and its chevron opens its leaves; a leaf narrows its item to the leaves ticked (the last leaf off drops the item, every leaf on collapses back to the whole). The value is one flat list of ids - an item's id for the whole item, leaf ids for a narrowed one. The search narrows the items by name, line or code and opens every group still holding one; opening the field starts the search afresh and opens the groups that already hold a pick. `nouns` supplies the words it counts with ("2 regions · 6 zones", "6 zones", "No regions selected") and `emptyText` the status line while nothing is picked, so nothing inside knows what it is picking. Built on the library's own parts: the Select trigger (the picks as small square Badges in the code face, "k/N" in blue when narrowed, "+N more" past `maxPills`), a Popover as wide as the field and 320 at least, the Input as the search, the ghost icon Button as the fold, the Checkbox as the pick (the unpicked box on the neutral-40 hairline), Clear all a ghost Button. The rows as the console draws them: 36 tall, 8 in, corners 6, the secondary wash under the pointer; a group's name 11/600 small capitals tracked 0.05em in neutral-90 with a 14 glyph before it and its "picked/all" count in the code face; an item's name 12/500 over its line 11 in the faint ink, its count 11/500 at the right; a leaf's name 12 on a 28 row; the guide lines - the spine 22 in (35 for leaves) on the neutral-20 hairline, a 16 branch into every row, the spine stopping at the last branch. Story: Select → Tree Select - shown with the cloud regions it was ported from. Ported from the cloud discovery profile's Provider & connection step (RegionSelect, Ronak 2026-10-15/16).
