Card's defaults change, and every existing Card picks this up. The corner goes from 8 to 12, the rule for cards in the console, which until now patched it on with a class. The `filled` and `outline` edge and the lines inside a card are now one colour, neutral-20 (#ECF1F9 / #1D2A3E), where `--mdt-border` drew #CAD3E2 in light. The header's sentence moves from `muted-foreground` to neutral-90, and the footer's note to neutral-60.
